Frequently Asked Questions

What does Trail Vault do?

Trail Vault is a powerful app designed for mortgage brokers to gain deeper insights into their commission income. It allows brokers to track and analyse both upfront and trail commission payments, view earnings on individual client loans, and segment their entire loan book by key metrics and more. With Trail Vault, brokers can make smarter business decisions backed by data, streamline revenue tracking, and unlock the full value of their client database.

How will this benefit my business?

Trail Vault helps mortgage brokers boost profitability and client retention by turning commission data into actionable insights. Track earnings, spot portfolio trends, and optimise your loan book with ease. Smart alerts keep you ahead of key loan changes and client milestones, so you can engage at the right time and maximise long-term value. With Trail Vault, you run a smarter, more efficient, and more client-focused business.

Is my data safe?

Yes, your data is protected with industry-standard security measures to ensure it remains safe and confidential. Trail Vault uses encrypted servers and secure protocols to safeguard all your information, and account access is secured with multi-factor authentication (including two-factor authentication) for an added layer of protection. We take data privacy seriously and continuously monitor our systems to keep your client and commission data secure at all times.

What kind of integrations are available?

Trail Vault integrates effortlessly with both Microsoft and Gmail platforms, allowing brokers to sync calendars, set automated reminders, and manage client communication directly from their preferred email and scheduling tools. Whether you're using Outlook or Google Workspace, Trail Vault works in the background to streamline workflows, schedule follow-ups, and ensure key client milestones are never missed — all without switching between multiple apps.

Controlled access for teams

Trail Vault offers flexible user permissions, giving head brokers full control over who sees what. Staff can be granted access to upload data, manage client information, and perform day-to-day tasks, while sensitive data like revenue figures, trail commissions, or client performance metrics can be restricted. This ensures your team stays productive without compromising confidentiality or business critical insights.
